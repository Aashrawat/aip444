# Credibility Report: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590

**Verdict: High credibility**

## Summary
This source is an arXiv preprint titled **“The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot.”** It reports a controlled experiment on software developers using GitHub Copilot and claims the treatment group completed a JavaScript HTTP server task **55.8% faster** than the control group.

## Author findings
Authors listed on the paper:
- Sida Peng
- Eirini Kalliamvakou
- Peter Cihon
- Mert Demirer

What I found about them:
- **Eirini Kalliamvakou** is a **Research Advisor at GitHub** and has a **PhD in Computer Science from the University of Victoria**.
- **Mert Demirer** is an **Associate Professor of Applied Economics at MIT Sloan**, with a **PhD from MIT**, and research focused on productivity and firm behavior.
- **Peter Cihon** has worked on **AI policy and standards** and has held senior-advisory roles related to AI standards/governance.
- **Sida Peng** has a research profile and publications visible via Google Scholar, but I did not find a concise institutional bio from the searches I ran.

Assessment: the author team has strong topic-relevant expertise across software engineering, productivity research, and AI policy. A caveat is that some authors have direct GitHub/Microsoft ties, which is relevant for conflict-of-interest context.

## Publication findings
- **Publication / site:** arXiv
- **Source type:** preprint server / self-published scholarly repository
- **Editorial process:** moderation, not peer review

What I found:
- arXiv is widely used and reputable for disseminating research quickly.
- It is **not a peer-reviewed journal**; submissions are moderated for topicality and scholarly form, but arXiv does not certify correctness.

## Claim verification
Main claim checked:
- Developers using GitHub Copilot finished the task **55.8% faster**.

Verification results:
- **Corroborated** by GitHub’s own post describing the same experiment and reporting the same approximate result.
- I did **not** find credible contradictions to the core result in the investigation performed here.
- The article itself provides an original experiment write-up and links a PDF and source materials, which supports it as a primary research source.

## Bias and currency notes
- The paper is a **primary research preprint**, so it is suitable for citing the study’s own findings.
- Because the experiment concerns a specific task and setup, the result should **not** be overgeneralized to all developer work or all AI coding tools.
- The presence of GitHub/Microsoft-affiliated authors means readers should keep an eye on possible incentive alignment, although that alone does not invalidate the result.
- Publication date: **submitted 13 Feb 2023**. The study is historical but still relevant as evidence about one controlled Copilot experiment.
